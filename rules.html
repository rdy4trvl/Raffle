Eligibility

Open to legal U.S. residents age 18 or older. One entry per person. Duplicate entries will be disqualified at time of drawing. Void where prohibited.

Entry Period

Entries accepted from 9:00 AM to 11:59 PM Pacific Time on the day of the event. Entries submitted outside the entry period will not be eligible.

How to Enter

Complete the entry form with your first name, last name, and email address, and submit.

Prize

One (1) free entry to the Marin Century on Saturday, August 1, 2026. Approximate retail value: $150.

Winner Selection

One winner will be selected at random from all eligible entries within seven (7) days of the event.

Winner Notification

The winner will be notified by email at the address provided at entry. The winner must respond within five (5) days of notification. If no response is received within that period, an alternate winner will be selected at random from remaining eligible entries.

Publicity

The winner's first name and last initial may be announced on marincyclists.com and Marin Cyclists social media channels.

Odds of Winning

Odds depend on the total number of eligible entries received.

Privacy

Entrant information and location will be used to administer this drawing and, where consent is given, for future Marin Cyclists communications. Every future marketing email will include an unsubscribe link. See the Marin Cyclists privacy policy link on marincyclists.com.
